read it as an ordering, not a verdict. on the 5,995 benchmark texts, a score under 0.6 was human 82% of the time, 0.6–0.7 was a coin (31% model), 0.7–0.8 leaned model (66%), and 0.8 and up was model 99% of the time. the scale is not a calibrated probability: 0.5 does not mean half.

under 50 words it is noise. 0.61 AUC between 25 and 50 words, where the average AI text scores 0.57 and the average human text 0.52. pangram declines these by its own scope statement; jev answers anyway, and the answer means little.

this is not a pangram replacement. held at a 1% false-positive rate it catches 62% of RAID's AI text; pangram publishes a 0.34% miss rate at a 0.004% false-positive rate. it flagged half of the real doctors' answers in HC3 as machine-written. never decide anything about a person with this.
